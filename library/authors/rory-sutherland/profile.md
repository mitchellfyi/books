# Rory Sutherland

## Who he is

Rory Sutherland is a British advertising executive, writer, speaker, and advocate for applying behavioural science to commercial and public problems. [Ogilvy](https://www.ogilvy.com/uk/about) lists him as President Emeritus of Ogilvy Consulting.

## Background and expertise

[TED](https://www.ted.com/speakers/rory_sutherland) says Sutherland taught classics before joining Ogilvy & Mather's planning department in 1988 and becoming a junior copywriter. Ogilvy says he later spent more than twenty years as a copywriter and creative director and helped establish its behavioural consulting work. He has served as President of the Institute of Practitioners in Advertising, spoken at TED, written The Spectator's Wiki Man column, and presented BBC Radio 4 series; Penguin and Ogilvy independently record several of those roles.

His expertise is practical: advertising, branding, direct response, creative problem-solving, and the use of psychological ideas in field interventions. He is skilled at identifying how framing, context, and small design changes affect behaviour. The reviewed sources do not establish that he is an academic psychologist or economist.

## Perspective and limitations

Sutherland writes as a practitioner who generates hypotheses from observed market behaviour. This provides memorable cases and attention to effects that formal plans overlook. It also means his work should not be treated as a systematic review or final scientific account. Technical claims need checking against subject specialists, and a successful case still needs evidence that it will transfer to another setting.

## Selected work

- *The Wiki Man* (2011): a collection of columns, interviews, posts, and reference material.
- *Alchemy* (2019): a book-length case for psychological value and counterintuitive experimentation.
