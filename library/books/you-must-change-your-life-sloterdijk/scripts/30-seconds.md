Peter Sloterdijk's provocation: religion does not exist. What exists are exercise systems — fasting, prayer, drill, repetition under a teacher — and the human is the animal that trains. Monks, athletes, musicians and soldiers are all doing one thing: rehearsing until the practice remakes the person. A genuinely useful reframing, restated for five hundred demanding pages without ever being tested. Five point four out of ten. Take the idea; the book is optional.
