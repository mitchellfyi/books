The Power of Moments argues that selected peaks, transitions, milestones, and endings shape memory more than ordinary minutes. Chip and Dan Heath offer four design elements: elevation through sensory distinction, stakes, or surprise; insight through discovery; pride through recognition, progress, and courage; and connection through shared meaning and responsive disclosure. Use the framework to serve participants, not manipulate them. A memorable ceremony cannot repair bad service, unsafe work, forced vulnerability, or an experience whose average quality is poor.
