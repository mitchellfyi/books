The Choice Factory follows one ordinary day and catches twenty-five psychological biases steering what you buy — social proof at the app store, charm pricing at the till, the pratfall effect in an honest ad. Adman Richard Shotton's twist: he field-tests each classic finding on real consumers before recommending it. The most immediately usable marketing-science book in print — a deck of tested starting hypotheses, best owned by anyone who prices or sells anything.
