The Let Them Theory turns one boundary into two instructions. Let other adults choose, judge, drift, or react; then ask, “Let me do what?” You might breathe, speak clearly, stop chasing, set a limit, or leave. The tool is memorable and useful for approval, friendship, and conflict, but it is old acceptance-and-agency wisdom stretched across 336 repetitive pages. Never use it to excuse danger, discrimination, neglected duties, or harm that needs repair.
