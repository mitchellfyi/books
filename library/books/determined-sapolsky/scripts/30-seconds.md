Determined says every choice grows from causes you did not choose: genes, development, stress, culture, opportunity, and the brain's immediate state. Robert Sapolsky argues that luck penetrates even grit, so protection and behaviour change remain necessary but hatred and deserved suffering do not. The causal science is powerful; his leap from caused behaviour to no meaningful agency is disputed. Read it for compassion and moral challenge, then test it against compatibilist accounts of voluntary control.
