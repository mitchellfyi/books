Nudge argues there's no neutral way to present a choice — some order, some default, some framing always steers — so designers should steer people toward their own goals while keeping every exit open: libertarian paternalism. Defaults are the proven core: automatic pension enrolment changed millions of retirements. It launched nudge units worldwide and a Nobel; the 2020s added the audit — average nudge effects are contested, defaults survive. Read it with that ledger attached.
