Influence is Robert Cialdini's map of the seven levers that make people say yes: reciprocation, commitment, social proof, liking, authority, scarcity, and unity. He learned them undercover, spending three years training in sales and telemarketing jobs, and wrote the book to arm the target, not the persuader. Still the definitive field guide to compliance tactics — though parts of its lab evidence, including its most famous anecdote, have been dismantled since 1984.
