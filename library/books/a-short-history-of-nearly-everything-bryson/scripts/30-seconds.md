A Short History of Nearly Everything is Bill Bryson's grand tour of science: the Big Bang, the weighing of the Earth, the atom, supervolcanoes, the story of life, and us, all told through the obsessives who figured it out. It remains the most painless introduction to science ever written, perfect for anyone school failed. Trust the wonder, not the details: specialists have catalogued its errors, and it is a two-thousand-three snapshot.
