Don't Believe Everything You Think is Joseph Nguyen's BookTok phenomenon, and its one idea is old and useful: suffering comes not from events, or even from thoughts, but from thinking — the elaboration you add. Notice thoughts without believing them, and peace surfaces on its own. Warm, simple, two million copies — and no evidence, no sources, no method. Therapists call it ancient material, oversold. The five-minute version genuinely is enough.
