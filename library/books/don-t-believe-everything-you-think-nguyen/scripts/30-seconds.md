Don't Believe Everything You Think argues that passing thoughts become suffering when we believe, judge and repeatedly elaborate them. Joseph Nguyen's useful advice is to notice the story your mind is building, pause, and choose whether it deserves attention. The book is warm and accessible, but turns this practical move into an unsupported theory of all psychological suffering. The expanded edition adds exercises, yet the central idea still fits comfortably in five minutes. Rating: three point nine out of ten.
