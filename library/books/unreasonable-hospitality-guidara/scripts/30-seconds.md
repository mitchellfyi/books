Unreasonable Hospitality, by restaurateur Will Guidara, tells how deliberate, extravagant care for guests took Eleven Madison Park from struggling brasserie to the world's best restaurant, and argues any business can choose the same: service is what you deliver, hospitality is how it feels. It is warm, practical, and full of famous gestures like the two-dollar hot dog served on fine china. Just know it is one triumphant memoir: the costs and failures barely appear.
