The Laws of Human Nature says people are less rational and transparent than they believe. Step back from emotion, judge character by repeated behaviour, practise empathy, preserve autonomy when persuading, resist group pressure, and organise life around purpose. Robert Greene makes eighteen patterns memorable through dramatic history, but selected stories cannot prove universal laws. Use the book as a checklist for questioning your first interpretation, never as permission to diagnose hidden motives from a gesture or analogy.
