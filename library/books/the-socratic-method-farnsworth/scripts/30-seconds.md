The Socratic Method, by law dean Ward Farnsworth, is a plain-language handbook for thinking the way Socrates questioned: elicit a belief, test it against the believer's other commitments, and treat being refuted as a gift. Built from hundreds of passages from Plato's dialogues, it suits lawyers, teachers, coaches, and anyone who works or lives by questions. Expect a patient manual with an ethic attached, not debate tactics, new scholarship, or practice drills.
