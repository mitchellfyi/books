Supercommunicators is Charles Duhigg's field manual for hard conversations. Talk moves among three modes — practical, emotional, and social — and fails when people answer different ones. Match the mode, prove you listened by asking, summarising, and checking, then ask about values instead of facts. Its best techniques can be practised today. Clear, usable, and built from real cases, though the science is familiar and the hardest conversations outrun the toolkit.
