Supercommunicators is Charles Duhigg's field manual for hard conversations. Every discussion is really one of three: practical, emotional, or social — and talk fails when two people are unknowingly having different ones. Match the conversation, prove you're listening by looping their words back, and ask about values instead of facts. Clear, immediately usable, and built from real cases — though the science is familiar and the hardest conversations outrun the toolkit.
