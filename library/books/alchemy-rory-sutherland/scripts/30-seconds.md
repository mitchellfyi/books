Alchemy, by advertising executive Rory Sutherland, argues that logic alone cannot predict human behaviour. Context, expectation, identity, and signals shape what people choose and value, so a small psychological change can beat an expensive physical improvement. It is a witty source of ideas for marketers, designers, entrepreneurs, and policy practitioners. Read it for better hypotheses, not settled science: its memorable anecdotes are stronger at provoking experiments than proving when a counterintuitive solution will work elsewhere.
