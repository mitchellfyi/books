The Power of Habit explains repeated behaviour as a loop: a cue triggers a routine that delivers an expected reward. Its best tool is practical: test what reward you actually seek, isolate the cue, then plan and rehearse a safer routine. It suits readers changing one recurring behaviour or auditing workplace systems. The core remains useful, but corporate stories overstate causation and its muscle-like theory of willpower has been weakened by later research. Content rating: 6.8 out of 10.
