How Not to Age argues that diet and lifestyle can delay disease and preserve function. Its reliable core is simple: eat varied, minimally processed plants, avoid smoking, stay active, build strength, sleep, connect, and use ordinary care before anti-ageing products. Michael Greger's pathway science and supplement scepticism are useful, but many food claims outrun human outcomes. Be especially cautious with calorie and protein restriction: theoretical longevity is not worth losing muscle, bone, healing, or adequate nutrition.
