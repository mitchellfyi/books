Why Nations Fail is the Nobel-winning institutions answer to global inequality: nations prosper under inclusive institutions that spread power and opportunity, and stay poor under extractive ones built to enrich elites — who rationally block the innovation that would threaten them. Geography and culture are dismissed; one city split by a fence is the proof. Powerful lens, famous critics — Gates, Sachs, Diamond — all pressing one fair point: it isn't the whole story.
