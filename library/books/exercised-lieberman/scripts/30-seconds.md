Exercised explains why knowing movement is healthy does not make us want it. Daniel Lieberman argues that humans evolved to conserve energy and move when necessary, social, or rewarding, not to perform optional workouts for distant benefits. Reluctance is normal; modern life with too little varied activity is the mismatch. Move often, combine aerobic and strength work, continue with age, and make it useful or enjoyable. It is strong science, though no ancestral comparison supplies one perfect routine.
