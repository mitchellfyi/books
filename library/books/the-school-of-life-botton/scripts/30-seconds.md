The School of Life argues that emotional maturity can be taught. Its curriculum covers self-knowledge, kindness toward other people, realistic love, meaningful work, and culture used for consolation. Its strongest lesson is that disappointment becomes easier when we stop demanding perfection from ourselves, partners, and careers. This is elegant applied philosophy, not evidence-based psychology or therapy. Read it for memorable reframes and a consoling voice; skip it if you need tested methods or exercises. Content rating: 5.5 out of 10.
