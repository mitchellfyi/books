The True Believer asks why people surrender themselves to mass causes. Eric Hoffer says a frustrated person may seek escape from an unwanted identity more than a particular doctrine. A movement supplies belonging, certainty, action, a glorious future, and an enemy. This sharp, compact theory explains recruitment and the shift from critics to fanatics to rulers. But selected history is not proof. Use Hoffer to question movements, including your own, never to diagnose opponents from afar.
