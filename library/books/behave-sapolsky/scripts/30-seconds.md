Behave is Robert Sapolsky's eight-hundred-page answer to why humans do anything: rewind the clock. A behaviour traces to neurons one second before, hormones hours before, adolescence years before, culture centuries before, evolution behind it all — never one cause. Testosterone doesn't cause aggression; there's no gene for anything interesting; our best and worst run on the same machinery. The genre's best-evidenced book, docked only for density and its 2017 vintage.
