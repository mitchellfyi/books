Deep Work argues that difficult learning and valuable creation require uninterrupted concentration, while email, meetings, feeds, and visible busyness consume attention without equivalent value. Cal Newport's four rules are to schedule demanding work, train yourself not to escape every bored moment, keep only digital tools that materially serve important goals, and contain administration. The method strongly suits writers, researchers, students, designers, and programmers. It needs adaptation when care, service, leadership, or rapid coordination is the valuable work.
