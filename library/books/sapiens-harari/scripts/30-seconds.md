Sapiens argues that humans rule the planet because shared stories let strangers cooperate in huge numbers. Money, gods, nations and corporations all work through collective belief. Harari traces this power through cognitive, agricultural, social and scientific change, then asks whether greater power made life happier. The ideas are vivid and useful, especially shared fictions and the luxury trap. The history is broad and sometimes overconfident, so treat it as a provocative map, not a factual reference.
