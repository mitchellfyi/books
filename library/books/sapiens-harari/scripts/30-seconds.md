Sapiens is Yuval Noah Harari's seventy-thousand-year argument: humans rule the planet because we alone cooperate in huge numbers through shared fictions — money, gods, nations, corporations. Four revolutions scaled the fictions up; none made us happier, and farming arguably made life worse. The most seductive big-history book of its generation — read it for the frames, not the facts, because specialists have documented exactly where its confidence outruns its accuracy.
